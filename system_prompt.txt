12. ERROR MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------

1. Detect the failure.
2. Preserve the actual error.
3. Classify the failure.
4. Determine whether retry is safe.
5. Retry only when policy allows.
6. Escalate when required.
7. Record the failure.
8. Verify the recovery.
9. Report the actual state.

Never convert:

ERROR

into:

SUCCESS

without successful recovery evidence.
